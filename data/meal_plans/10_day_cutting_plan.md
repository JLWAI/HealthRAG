## How to use this plan

Each day has 4 meal slots: **Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Evening Snack**. Each slot has a **meal pool** of 4-5 interchangeable options that hit similar macros (±5g protein, ±8g carbs, ±5g fat). Swap any meal within its slot without blowing your daily totals.

**Swap rules:**
- Within a slot → always fine (macros match)
- Breakfast ↔ Evening Snack → fine (both lower-carb)
- Lunch ↔ Dinner → fine (both moderate-carb, post-workout options)
- Don't swap across carb tiers (e.g. high-carb lunch into evening slot) without rebalancing

**Carb distribution by slot** (intentional — puts carbs around training):
- Breakfast: ~20g carbs (lower — AM insulin sensitivity)
- Lunch: ~45g carbs (highest — pre/post workout fuel)
- Dinner: ~40g carbs (high — recovery)
- Evening snack: ~25g carbs (lower — wind down, casein for overnight)

## Macro Timing Strategy

**Training days** (Day 1, 3, 4, 6, 8, 9): Use higher-carb options
- Breakfast: B3 Protein Oats (40g C)
- Lunch: L1, L3, L5 (45-50g C pre/post workout)
- Dinner: D2, D5 (40-45g C for recovery)
- Snack: S4 Yogurt Parfait (25g C)

**Rest days** (Day 2, 5, 7, 10): Shift toward lower-carb options
- Breakfast: B4 Cottage Cheese or B5 Southwest Bake (15-20g C)
- Lunch: L2 or L4 (balanced)
- Dinner: D4 Turkey Meatballs (35g C)
- Snack: S5 Egg Whites & Jerky (6g C)

## Notes

- **Hydration**: 1 gallon water/day minimum. Coffee/tea fine, no liquid calories.
- **Flex 200 cal**: If you miss your target, use it for more veggies, an extra tbsp peanut butter, or a small piece of dark chocolate. Don't go over.
- **Alcohol**: Treat as carbs + fat. 1 light beer = cut 1 serving of rice/oats that day.
- **Weekly check-in**: Log body weight daily (AM, post-bathroom, pre-food). Use the Adaptive TDEE system to review trend weight every 7 days. Adjust calories only after 2 weeks of data.
- **Expected rate**: ~1 lb/week loss on 2,034 cal at 221 lbs. 21 lbs in 12 weeks = on track for 200 lb goal.
